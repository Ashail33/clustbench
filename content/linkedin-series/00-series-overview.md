# LinkedIn article series: clustbench

A 5-post series written for LinkedIn (article/long-post format, roughly
300 to 500 words each), personal-narrative voice matching the author's
existing Medium presence. Distinct from `content/linkedin-posts.md`
(atomised hooks for the Medium series).

## Series arc

1. `01-reflections-and-why-data-matters.md`
   Personal opener. The master's journey, the moment I realised that
   the data pipeline underneath a model matters more than the model.

2. `02-the-database-i-proposed.md`
   The centralised clustering-research database I argued for in the
   thesis, and why nobody had built one.

3. `03-the-data-model.md`
   What that database actually looks like in practice. The schema
   (Task, Record, StepRecord) with real code.

4. `04-automating-algorithm-discovery.md`
   The end-to-end pipeline: dataset generator, harness, metrics,
   trajectory capture, dashboard.

5. `05-self-extending-discovery.md`
   Fifteen rounds later: how the system now finds better algorithms
   without me writing router code.

## Style rules (author preferences)

- Personal narrative openings ("A few years ago I ...").
- First person, contractions, plain vocabulary.
- Concrete numbers where they help.
- No em-dashes. No spaced-hyphens as substitutes.
- End with the dashboard + repo links.
- 300 to 500 words each.

## Cadence

Post one per week for five weeks. Each drives back to the same two
links: https://github.com/Ashail33/clustbench and
https://ashail33.github.io/clustbench/.
